Evaluation Results

This page presents the findings from the user study comparing human-written and AI-generated data stories.

Study Overview

This study evaluates the effectiveness of human-written and AI-generated data stories created from the Austin Animal Center Outcomes dataset.

📊 Dataset

Austin Animal Center Outcomes dataset, focusing on pet adoption trends during the COVID-19 period and animal abandonment trends in the following years.

⚖️ Comparison

Participants compared a human-written story with an AI-generated story based on the same dataset.

👥 Participants

Users were asked to read both stories and provide feedback through an evaluation questionnaire.

🎯 Evaluation Criteria

  • Clarity
  • Emotional Engagement
  • Trustworthiness
  • Overall Quality

🔍 Key Findings

The evaluation highlights several interesting differences between human-written and AI-generated storytelling approaches.

💙 Stronger Emotional Impact

Participants generally found the human-written story more emotionally engaging and relatable.

🤖 Structured AI Narrative

The AI-generated story was perceived as well-organized and effective at presenting data-driven information.

⚖️ Similar Trust Levels

Both stories achieved comparable trustworthiness scores, suggesting that AI can communicate factual information reliably.

🏆 Overall Preference

Most participants preferred the human-written story due to its stronger narrative flow and emotional connection.
